Help and contact

How do I publish a listing?

Create an account, click Publish listing, fill in the form and add a photo. Our team reviews it before it appears.

Why is my listing not showing?

New and edited listings wait for review. You can see the status in My listings.

How do I mark something as sold?

Open My listings and click Mark as sold.

How do I report a problem?

Open the listing and click Report this listing.

My listing was hidden. Can I appeal?

Yes. Send us a message, choose the topic Appeal a decision about my listing, name the listing and explain why it should be restored.

Contact the team

Logged-in users can send us a message for questions or problems with the site.
