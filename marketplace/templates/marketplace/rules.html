Marketplace rules

What you can post

Real services and items that you own or are able to provide, with an honest description, a fair price and your correct contact number.

What is not allowed

  • Fake, misleading or duplicate listings
  • Stolen, illegal or dangerous items and services
  • Asking buyers to pay before they see the item or the work
  • Photos or text that belong to someone else
  • Abusive or offensive content

How listings are reviewed

Every new listing is checked by our team before it appears publicly. If you edit an approved listing, it is checked again. Listings that break these rules are removed.

Reports

If you see a problem, use Report this listing on the listing page. Listings that receive several reports are hidden until our team reviews them.

Stay safe

  • Never pay before you see the item or the work.
  • Meet in a public place and bring a friend if you can.
  • Check the item before you pay.
