Privacy notice

KWAREWA is a test version built as a student project.

What we store

  • Your username and a protected (hashed) password. We cannot read your password.
  • The listings you publish: title, description, price, location, photos and contact numbers.
  • Reports you send about listings.

What other people can see

The phone and WhatsApp numbers you put in a listing are public once the listing is approved. Anyone who opens the listing can see them. Only enter numbers you are happy to share.

Cookies

We use a login cookie so that you stay signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any of your listings from My listings. To remove your account, contact us through Help and contact.
